WELCOME TO KIBANDA SECONDARY SCHOOL

Education For Nation Building

Kibanda Secondary School is dedicated to providing quality education, nurturing discipline, and developing future leaders. We strive to create a safe and supportive learning environment where students can achieve academic excellence, build strong character, and prepare for successful careers and responsible citizenship.

School Overview

Kibanda Secondary School is a center of academic excellence dedicated to providing quality education, nurturing character, and developing responsible citizens. Since its establishment, the school has remained committed to empowering learners with knowledge, skills, and values necessary for success in a rapidly changing world.

The school offers a supportive and inclusive learning environment where students are encouraged to achieve their full potential academically, socially, and morally. Through a combination of experienced teachers, modern teaching methods, and a strong emphasis on discipline, the school continues to produce confident, innovative, and productive individuals.

In addition to academics, the school promotes leadership, sports, cultural activities, and community engagement, ensuring the holistic development of every learner. Our mission is to inspire lifelong learning, integrity, and excellence while preparing students to become future leaders and positive contributors to society.

Ordinary Level Fees Details

O-Level Day Scholars Requirements & Fees

The following is the fee structure and requirements for new O-Level day scholars. Lunch is optional and students may choose to either pay or provide food items in kind.

S.1 – S.2

Total Fee: UGX 150,000

S.3 – S.4

Total Fee: UGX 180,000

School Uniform (Purchased at School)

Students are required to buy school uniform from the school. This includes: trousers or skirts, T-shirt, and sweater at a total cost of UGX 120,000.

Lunch (Optional)

Lunch contribution is UGX 110,000 per term.
Alternatively, students may bring in-kind food items:
• 10kg beans
• 20kg maize grains
• UGX 10,000 cash contribution

School Requirements (In Kind)

Each student is expected to bring:
• 1 broom
• 1 ream of photocopying paper
• 1 slasher (for cleaning and compound maintenance)

O-Level Boarding Students Requirements & Fees

This section outlines the fees and requirements for O-Level boarding students. It includes tuition, accommodation, meals, and essential school needs.

S.1 – S.2

Total Fee: UGX 750,000

S.3

Total Fee: UGX 780,000

S.4

Total Fee: UGX 820,000

School Uniform (Purchased at School)

Boarding students are required to purchase school uniform from the school at a cost of UGX 120,000. This includes trousers/skirts, T-shirts, and sweaters.

Meals (Included / Alternative Option)

Meals are provided at school under the boarding package. However, students may alternatively contribute:
• 10kg beans
• 20kg maize grains
• UGX 10,000 cash contribution

School Requirements (In Kind)

Boarding students must bring:
• 1 broom
• 1 ream of photocopying paper
• 1 slasher

Personal Requirements

Students should come with:
• Mattress, bedsheets, and blanket
• Mosquito net
• Trunk/suitcase
• Plate, cup, and spoon
• Toiletries (soap, toothpaste, toothbrush)
• Torch/flashlight
• School bag and stationery

O-Level Subjects

O-Level Academic Structure

The O-Level academic program is organized into departments to ensure effective teaching, learning, and subject specialization.

Science Department

This department focuses on developing scientific knowledge and practical skills.

Subjects: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Agriculture

Humanities Department

This department helps students understand society, history, and environment.

Subjects: History, Geography, CRE/IRE, Literature in English

Languages Department

This department strengthens communication and language skills.

Subjects: English Language, Luganda (or Local Language), Literature

Practical & Vocational Department

This department equips students with ICT and life skills for the modern world.

Subjects: ICT, Entrepreneurship, Computer Studies, Life Skills

Academic Goal

The O-Level program aims to build a strong foundation for students to succeed in national examinations (UCE) and prepare them for advanced studies at A-Level.

A-Level Subjects

A-Level Academic Structure

Kibanda Secondary School offers a well-structured A-Level programme designed to prepare learners for higher education and professional careers. Students are guided to choose subjects in either Sciences or Arts based on their abilities and career goals.

Science Department

At Kibanda Secondary School, the Science Department equips learners with strong analytical and practical skills required in science-related careers. Learners are trained through both theory and practical work in well-organized laboratory sessions.

Subjects Offered: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, ICT

Arts Department

The Arts Department at Kibanda Secondary School focuses on developing communication, critical thinking, and leadership skills. It prepares learners for careers in education, law, administration, and social sciences.

Subjects Offered: History, Geography, Economics, Literature in English, Divinity, Entrepreneurship, General Paper

Academic Guidance

Kibanda Secondary School provides continuous academic guidance to help students select subject combinations that match their future careers. Regular assessments, teacher support, and academic follow-up ensure that every learner performs to the best of their ability.

Results

Kibanda Secondary School Results

Kibanda Secondary School is proud of its academic performance in national examinations. Below is a summary of our O-Level and A-Level results.

O-Level (UCE) Results

Performance Summary

Kibanda Secondary School continues to record improved performance in UCE examinations. Most students pass in Division 1 and Division 2.

Key Highlights

• High pass rate in sciences and mathematics
• Improved English and ICT performance
• Strong discipline contributing to academic success

A-Level (UACE) Results

Performance Summary

Our A-Level students perform well in national examinations with many qualifying for university admission each year.

Key Highlights

• Strong performance in science combinations (PCM, PCB)
• Improved arts results in humanities subjects
• Increased university admission rate
